Hi there! I’m Tianlin. 

I’m a research scientist on the Gemma team at Google DeepMind. I obtained my PhD with [Prof. Ivan Dokmanić](https://dmi.unibas.ch/de/personen/ivan-dokmanic/) at the University of Basel in Switzerland. I received BSc (2016) and MSc (2019) both from Jacobs University Bremen; during my time there, I completed my master's thesis under the guidance of [Prof. Herbert Jaeger](https://www.ai.rug.nl/minds/).

### News

* **Nov 2024**: I defended my PhD thesis and joined Google DeepMind.

* **May 2024**: Our [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02992) on decoding-time realignment of language models was accepted by ICML 2024 as a spotlight presentation.

* **April 2024**: Our [paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=aHk3vctnf1) on evaluating routers in vision mixture-of-experts was accepted by TMLR 2024.
